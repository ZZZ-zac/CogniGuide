2) Let $X_1 = \{u_1, u_2, \ldots, u_n\}$  
$X_2 = \{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_n\}$  

Given that $X_1$ and $X_2$ are subspaces of $V$,  
$\because X_1$ is a subspace of $V$  
$\therefore u_i + u_j \in X_1$, $\forall\ 1 \le i, j \le n$  
and $\alpha_i u_i \in X_1$, $\forall\ 1 \le i \le n$

$\because X_2$ is a subspace of $V$  
$\therefore v_i + v_j \in X_2$, $\forall\ 1 \le i, j \le n$  
and $\beta_i v_i \in X_2$, $\forall\ 1 \le i \le n$

$\alpha_i u_i \in X_1$, $\beta_i v_i \in X_2$, $\forall\ 1 \le i \le n$  
$\Rightarrow \alpha_i u_i + \beta_i v_i \in X_1 + X_2$, $\forall\ 1 \le i \le n$
