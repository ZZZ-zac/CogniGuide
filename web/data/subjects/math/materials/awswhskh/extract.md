1. In $\triangle ABC$, $B = 30^\circ$, $C = 20^\circ$ and $a = 9$ cm. Solve the triangle.

$$
\begin{aligned}
A + B + C &= 180^\circ \\
A + 30^\circ + 20^\circ &= 180^\circ \\
A &= 130^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$

By the sine formula,
$$
\frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{a}{\sin A}
$$
$$
\frac{b}{\sin 30^\circ} = \frac{9 \text{ cm}}{\sin 130^\circ}
$$
$$
b = \frac{9 \sin 30^\circ}{\sin 130^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$
$$
= 5.87 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$

$$
\frac{c}{\sin C} = \frac{a}{\sin A}
$$
$$
\frac{c}{\sin 20^\circ} = \frac{9 \text{ cm}}{\sin 130^\circ}
$$
$$
c = \frac{9 \sin 20^\circ}{\sin 130^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$
$$
= 4.02 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$

3. In $\triangle ABC$, $A = 50^\circ$ and $B = 25^\circ$.

Explain  
(a) Is it true that $BC = 2AC$? Explain your answer.  
(b) If $BC = 10$ cm, is it possible that $AB > 13$ cm? Explain your answer.

(a) By the sine formula,
$$
\frac{BC}{\sin A} = \frac{AC}{\sin B}
$$
$$
\frac{BC}{AC} = \frac{\sin A}{\sin B}
$$
$$
= \frac{\sin 50^\circ}{\sin 25^\circ}
$$
$$
= 1.81, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$
$\because \quad 1.81 \ne 2$  
$\therefore \quad$ It is not true that $BC = 2AC$.

(b) 
$$
\begin{aligned}
A + B + C &= 180^\circ \\
50^\circ + 25^\circ + C &= 180^\circ \\
C &= 105^\circ
\end{aligned}
$$

By the sine formula,
$$
\frac{AB}{\sin C} = \frac{BC}{\sin A}
$$
$$
\frac{AB}{\sin 105^\circ} = \frac{10 \text{ cm}}{\sin 50^\circ}
$$
$$
AB = \frac{10 \sin 105^\circ}{\sin 50^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$
$$
= 12.6 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$
$\because \quad 12.6 \text{ cm} < 13 \text{ cm}$  
$\therefore \quad$ It is not possible that $AB > 13$ cm.
