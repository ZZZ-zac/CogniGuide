$$  
$$
\sin B = \frac{9 \sin 42^\circ}{12} = 6.8

25. (a) Construct $EA$ such that $EA \parallel CB$, where $E$ is a point on $DC$.  
[Diagram: quadrilateral ABCD with AB = 3.5 cm, BC = 3.6 cm, angle at C = 72°, angle at D = 66°, and EA parallel to CB, intersecting DC at E.]

$$
\angle AED = \angle BCD = 72^\circ \quad (\text{corr. } \angle s, EA \parallel CB)
$$  
$$
AE = BC = 3.6 \text{ cm} \quad (\text{opp. sides of //gram})
$$  
In $\triangle ADE$, by the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{AD}{\sin 72^\circ} = \frac{AE}{\sin 65^\circ}
$$  
$$
AD = \frac{3.6 \sin 72^\circ}{\sin 65^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
= 3.78 \text{ cm, cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  

(b) In $\triangle ADE$,  
$$
\angle DAE + \angle AED + \angle ADC = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
\angle DAE + 72^\circ + 65^\circ = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
\angle DAE = 43^\circ
$$  

By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{AE}{\sin 43^\circ} = \frac{DE}{\sin 65^\circ}
$$  
$$
DE = \frac{3.6 \sin 43^\circ}{\sin 65^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
= 2.709 \text{ cm, cor. to } 5 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
EC = AB - AE = (18 - 8) \text{ cm} = 10 \text{ cm}

$$
DC = DE + EC = (2.709 + 3.5) \text{ cm} = 6.21 \text{ cm, cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
