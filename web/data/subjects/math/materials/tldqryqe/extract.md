Solve the following triangles. [Nos. 7–9]  

7.  
![](https://i.imgur.com/7.png)  
$A + B + C = 180^\circ$  
$35^\circ + B + 88^\circ = 180^\circ$  
$B = \underline{57^\circ}$  
By the sine formula,  
$\dfrac{AC}{\sin 57^\circ} = \dfrac{31 \text{ cm}}{\sin 88^\circ}$  
$AC = \dfrac{31 \sin 57^\circ}{\sin 88^\circ} \text{ cm}$  
$= 26.0 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$  
$\dfrac{BC}{\sin 35^\circ} = \dfrac{31 \text{ cm}}{\sin 88^\circ}$  
$BC = \dfrac{31 \sin 35^\circ}{\sin 88^\circ} \text{ cm}$  
$= 17.8 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$  

8.  
![](https://i.imgur.com/8.png)  
$A + B + C = 180^\circ$  
$123^\circ + 22^\circ + C = 180^\circ$  
$C = \underline{35^\circ}$  
By the sine formula,  
$\dfrac{AC}{\sin 22^\circ} = \dfrac{19 \text{ cm}}{\sin 35^\circ}$  
$AC = \dfrac{19 \sin 22^\circ}{\sin 35^\circ} \text{ cm}$  
$= 12.4 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$  
$\dfrac{BC}{\sin 123^\circ} = \dfrac{19 \text{ cm}}{\sin 35^\circ}$  
$BC = \dfrac{19 \sin 123^\circ}{\sin 35^\circ} \text{ cm}$  
$= 27.8 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$  

9.  
![](https://i.imgur.com/9.png)
