7. In $\triangle ABC$, $B = 30^\circ$ and $AB = 20$ cm.

Explain (a) Kelvin claims that the minimum length of $AC$ is 10 cm. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

(b) Find the range of the length of $AC$ such that two triangles can be formed.

(a) When $AC \perp BC$, the length of $AC$ is minimum and  
$$
\sin 30^\circ = \frac{AC}{20 \text{ cm}}
$$  
$$
AC = 20 \sin 30^\circ \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
= 10 \text{ cm}
$$  
$\therefore$ The claim is agreed.

(b) When two triangles can be formed,  
$$
10 \text{ cm} < AC < AB
$$  
$$
10 \text{ cm} < AC < 20 \text{ cm}
$$  

**Supplementary Example 4** Level 2 (P.9.8)  
In each of the following, use the sine formula to find $B$ in $\triangle ABC$.

(a) $A = 40^\circ$, $b = 7$ cm, $a = 5$ cm  
(b) $A = 40^\circ$, $b = 7$ cm, $a = 10$ cm  

(Give the answers correct to 3 significant figures.)

(a) By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{7 \text{ cm}}{\sin B} = \frac{5 \text{ cm}}{\sin 40^\circ}
$$  
$$
\sin B = \frac{7 \sin 40^\circ}{5}
$$  
$$
B = 64.145^\circ \text{ or } 180^\circ - 64.145^\circ
$$  
$\therefore$ $B = 64.1^\circ$ or $116^\circ$, cor. to 3 sig. fig.  

(An example of Case 3)

(b) By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{7 \text{ cm}}{\sin B} = \frac{10 \text{ cm}}{\sin 40^\circ}
$$  
$$
\sin B = \frac{7 \sin 40^\circ}{10}
$$  
$$
B = 26.7^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  

(An example of Case 4)

**Supplementary Example 5** Level 2 (P.9.9)  
Solve $\triangle ABC$ in each of the following.

(a) $A = 120^\circ$, $b = 9$ cm, $a = 15$ cm  
(b) $A = 120^\circ$, $b = 9$ cm, $a = 5\sqrt{3}$ cm  

(Give the answers correct to 3 significant figures.)

(a) By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{9 \text{ cm}}{\sin B} = \frac{15 \text{ cm}}{\sin 120^\circ}
$$  
$$
\sin B = \frac{9 \sin 120^\circ}{15}
$$  
$$
B = 31.3^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
A + B + C = 180^\circ \quad (\angle \text{ sum of } \triangle)
$$  
$$
120^\circ + 31.306^\circ + C = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
C = 28.7^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  
By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{c}{\sin 28.694^\circ} = \frac{15 \text{ cm}}{\sin 120^\circ}
$$  
$$
c = \frac{15 \sin 28.694^\circ}{\sin 120^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
= 8.32 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  

(An example of Case 6)

(b) By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{9 \text{ cm}}{\sin B} = \frac{5\sqrt{3} \text{ cm}}{\sin 120^\circ}
$$  
$$
\sin B = \frac{9 \sin 120^\circ}{5\sqrt{3}}
$$  
$$
B = 64.158^\circ, \text{ cor. to 5 sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
A + B = 120^\circ + 64.158^\circ
$$  
$$
= 184.158^\circ
$$  
$$
> 180^\circ
$$  
i.e. $\triangle ABC$ cannot be formed.  
$\therefore$ There is no solution.

(An example of Case 5)
