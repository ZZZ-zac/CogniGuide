③ 12. In the figure, $ PQ = 20\,\text{cm} $, $ QR = 7\,\text{cm} $ and $ PR = 15\,\text{cm} $.  
(a) Find the area of $ \triangle PQR $. $ 42\,\text{cm}^2 $  
(b) Find the height $ h $ of $ \triangle PQR $. ⇨ Example 18 $ 5.6\,\text{cm} $  

12. (a) $ s = \frac{1}{2}(7 + 15 + 20)\,\text{cm} = 21\,\text{cm} $  
By Heron’s formula,  
area of $ \triangle PQR $  
$ = \sqrt{21(21 - 7)(21 - 15)(21 - 20)}\,\text{cm}^2 $  
$ = 42\,\text{cm}^2 $  

(b) Area of $ \triangle PQR = \frac{1}{2} \times PR \times h\,\text{cm}^2 $  
$ h = \frac{2 \times \text{area of } \triangle PQR}{PR} $  
$ = \frac{2 \times 42}{15}\,\text{cm} $  
$ = 5.6\,\text{cm} $  

③ 13. In the figure, $ AB = 9\,\text{cm} $, $ BC = 15\,\text{cm} $ and $ AC = 8\,\text{cm} $.  
(a) Find the area of $ \triangle ABC $. $ 29.9\,\text{cm}^2 $  
(b) Find the height from $ A $ to $ BC $. $ 3.99\,\text{cm} $  

13. (a) $ s = \frac{1}{2}(15 + 8 + 9)\,\text{cm} = 16\,\text{cm} $  
By Heron’s formula,  
area of $ \triangle ABC $  
$ = \sqrt{16(16 - 15)(16 - 8)(16 - 9)}\,\text{cm}^2 $  
$ = 29.9\,\text{cm}^2 $, cor. to 3 sig. fig.  
$ 29.933 $  

(b) Let $ h\,\text{cm} $ be the height from $ A $ to $ BC $.  
Area of $ \triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2} \times 15 \times h\,\text{cm}^2 $  
$ h = \frac{2 \times \text{area of } \triangle ABC}{BC} $  
$ = \frac{2 \times 29.933}{15} $  
$ = 3.99 $, cor. to 3 sig. fig.  
∴ The height from $ A $ to $ BC $ is $ 3.99\,\text{cm} $.
