26. $\angle BAD = 90^\circ$  

$$
\angle CAD = \angle BAD - \angle BAC
$$  
$$
= 90^\circ - 38^\circ
$$  
$$
= 52^\circ
$$  

In $\triangle ACD$,  
$$
\tan \angle CAD = \frac{CD}{AD}
$$  
$$
CD = 8 \tan 52^\circ \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
AB = CD = 8 \tan 52^\circ \text{ cm}
$$  

In $\triangle ABE$, by the cosine formula,  
$$
BE^2 = AB^2 + AE^2 - 2 \times AB \times AE \times \cos \angle BAE
$$  
$$
BE = \sqrt{(8 \tan 52^\circ)^2 + 7^2 - 2 \times 8 \tan 52^\circ \times 7 \times \cos 38^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
= 6.39 \text{ cm, cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  

28. (a) In $\triangle PQS$,  
$$
\angle PSQ + \angle QPS + \angle PQS = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
\angle PSQ + 59^\circ + 64^\circ = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
\angle PSQ = 57^\circ
$$  

By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{QS}{\sin 59^\circ} = \frac{7.5 \text{ cm}}{\sin 57^\circ}
$$  
$$
QS = \frac{7.5 \sin 59^\circ}{\sin 57^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$  
$$
= 7.67 \text{ cm, cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{7.6654}
$$  

(b) In $\triangle QRS$, by the cosine formula,  
$$
\cos \angle QRS = \frac{QR^2 + RS^2 - QS^2}{2 \times QR \times RS}
$$  
$$
= \frac{7^2 + 3^2 - 7.6654^2}{2 \times 7 \times 3}
$$  
$$
\angle QRS = 91.035^\circ, \text{ cor. to } 5 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
\angle QPS + \angle QRS = 59^\circ + 91.035^\circ
$$  
$$
= 150^\circ, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
\ne 180^\circ
$$  
$$
\therefore \quad PQRS \text{ is not a cyclic quadrilateral.}
$$
