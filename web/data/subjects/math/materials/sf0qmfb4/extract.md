1. (a) Area of $\triangle ABC$  
$$
= \frac{1}{2} \times AB \times AC \times \sin A \\
= \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 15 \times \sin 52^\circ \text{ cm}^2 \\

(b) $EF = DE = 7$ cm

$$
= \frac{1}{2} \times DE \times EF \times \sin E \\
= \frac{1}{2} \times 7 \times 7 \times \sin 100^\circ \text{ cm}^2 \\
= 24.1 \text{ cm}^2, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}

(c) Note that $\triangle PQR$ is an equilateral triangle,

③ 3. (a) $\theta$ is an acute angle.  
(b) $\theta$ is an obtuse angle.

3. (a) Area of $\triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2} \times AB \times AC \times \sin A$  
$$
8 = \frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 4 \times \sin \theta \\
\sin \theta = \frac{4}{5} \\
\theta = 53.130^\circ \text{ or } 180^\circ - 53.130^\circ \text{ (reject)} \\
= \underline{53.1^\circ}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$

(b) Area of $\triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2} \times AC \times BC \times \sin C$  
$$
50 = \frac{1}{2} \times 12 \times 9 \times \sin \theta \\
\sin \theta = \frac{25}{27} \\
\theta = 180^\circ - 67.808^\circ \text{ or } 67.808^\circ \text{ (reject)} \\
= \underline{112^\circ}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$

(c) Area of $\triangle ABC = \frac{1}{2} \times AC \times BC \times \sin C$  
$$
189 = \frac{1}{2} \times 21 \times 18 \times \sin \theta \\
\sin \theta = 1 \\
\theta = \underline{90^\circ}
