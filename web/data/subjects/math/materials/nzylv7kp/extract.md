30. In the figure, $ABCD$ is a square of side 2 cm. $AEB$ is a semi-circle with centre $O$ and $AEC$ is a quarter of circle with centre $D$.

(a) Prove that $OADE$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.

(b) Find $\cos \angle ADE$. $\frac{3}{5}$

(c) Find the area of the shaded region. $0.962\,\text{cm}^2$

30. (a) Join $AE$.

$\angle DAO = 90^\circ$ \quad (property of square)  
$\angle DAE + \angle OAE = 90^\circ$  
In $\triangle AOE$,  
$\because\; OA = OE$  
$\therefore\; \angle OEA = \angle OAE$ \quad (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)  
In $\triangle ADE$,  
$\because\; DA = DE$  
$\therefore\; \angle AED = \angle DAE$ \quad (base $\angle$s, isos. $\triangle$)  
$\therefore\; \angle OEA + \angle AED = \angle DAE + \angle OAE$  
$\angle OED = 90^\circ$  
$\angle OAD + \angle OED$ \quad , cor. to  
$= 90^\circ + 90^\circ$ \quad 5 sig. fig.  
$= 180^\circ$  
$\therefore\; OADE$ is a cyclic quadrilateral.  
\quad (opp. $\angle$s supp.)

(b) $\angle AOE + \angle ADE = 180^\circ$  
$\angle AOE = 180^\circ - \angle ADE$  
In $\triangle AOE$,  
$\because\; AB = 2$ cm and  
$\quad O$ is the centre of the circle $ABE$.  
$\therefore\; OA = OE = OB = 1$ cm  
By the cosine formula,  
$AE^2 = OA^2 + OE^2 - 2 \times OA \times OE \times \cos \angle AOE$  
$AE = \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2 - 2 \times 1 \times 1 \times \cos (180^\circ - \angle ADE)}$  
$\quad = \sqrt{2 + 2 \cos \angle ADE}$  
In $\triangle ADE$,  
$DA = DE = 2$ cm  
By the cosine formula,  
$AE^2 = DA^2 + DE^2 - 2 \times DA \times DE \times \cos \angle ADE$  
$AE = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2 - 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times \cos \angle ADE}$  
$\quad = \sqrt{8 - 8 \cos \angle ADE}$  
$AE = AE$  
$\sqrt{2 + 2 \cos \angle ADE} = \sqrt{8 - 8 \cos \angle ADE}$  
$2 + 2 \cos \angle ADE = 8 - 8 \cos \angle ADE$  
$10 \cos \angle ADE = 6$  
$\cos \angle ADE = \frac{3}{5}$

(c) $\sin^2 \angle ADE + \cos^2 \angle ADE = 1$  
$\sin \angle ADE = \pm \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2}$  
$\quad = \frac{4}{5} \text{ or } -\frac{4}{5} \text{ (rejected)}$  

$\sin \angle AOE$  
$= \sin (180^\circ - \angle ADE)$  
$= \sin \angle ADE$  
$= \frac{4}{5}$  

$\sin \angle AOE = \frac{4}{5}$  
$\angle AOE = 53.130^\circ \text{ (rejected) or } 126.87^\circ$  

$\therefore\; \angle ADE = 53.130^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.
