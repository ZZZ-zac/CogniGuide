# Solution of HW 9.2

19. (a) By the cosine formula,  
$$c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos C$$  
$$c = \sqrt{14^2 + 20^2 - 2 \times 14 \times 20 \times \cos 72^\circ} \text{ cm}$$  
$$= 20.6 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{20.566}$$  

By the cosine formula,  
$$\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$$  
$$= \frac{20^2 + 20.566^2 - 14^2}{2 \times 20 \times 20.566}$$  
$$A = 40.3^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{40.347}$$  
$$A + B + C = 180^\circ$$  
$$40.347^\circ + B + 72^\circ = 180^\circ$$  
$$B = 67.7^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$$  

(b) By the cosine formula,  
$$\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$$  
$$= \frac{15^2 + 18^2 - 12^2}{2 \times 15 \times 18}$$  
$$A = 41.4^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{41.410}$$  
$$\cos B = \frac{c^2 + a^2 - b^2}{2ca}$$  
$$= \frac{18^2 + 12^2 - 15^2}{2 \times 18 \times 12}$$  
$$B = 55.8^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{55.771}$$  
$$A + B + C = 180^\circ$$  
$$41.410^\circ + 55.771^\circ + C = 180^\circ$$  
$$C = 82.8^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$$  

21. (a) In $\triangle ABC$, by the cosine formula,  
$$\cos \angle ABC = \frac{AB^2 + BC^2 - AC^2}{2 \times AB \times BC}$$  
$$= \frac{8^2 + 17^2 - 12^2}{2 \times 8 \times 17}$$  
$$\angle ABC = 39.8^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{39.791}$$  

(b) In $\triangle ABD$, by the sine formula,  
$$\frac{AD}{\sin 39.791^\circ} = \frac{8 \text{ cm}}{\sin 50^\circ}$$  
$$AD = \frac{8 \sin 39.791^\circ}{\sin 50^\circ} \text{ cm}$$  
$$= 6.68 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$$  

22. Join $BD$.  

In $\triangle ABD$, by Pythagoras’ theorem,  
$$BD^2 = AB^2 + AD^2$$  
$$BD = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} \text{ cm}$$  
$$= 13 \text{ cm}$$  

In $\triangle BCD$, by the cosine formula,  
$$\cos \angle BCD = \frac{CD^2 + BC^2 - BD^2}{2 \times CD \times BC}$$  
$$= \frac{8^2 + 9^2 - 13^2}{2 \times 8 \times 9}$$  
$$\angle BCD = 99.6^\circ, \text{ cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$$  

23. (a) In $\triangle ABD$,  
$$\angle ABD + \angle BAD + \angle ADB = 180^\circ$$  
$$\angle ABD + 104^\circ + 43^\circ = 180^\circ$$  
$$\angle ABD = 33^\circ$$  

By the sine formula,  
$$\frac{BD}{\sin 104^\circ} = \frac{6 \text{ cm}}{\sin 33^\circ}$$  
$$BD = \frac{6 \sin 104^\circ}{\sin 33^\circ} \text{ cm}$$  
$$= 10.7 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{10.689}$$  

(b) $\angle DBC = \angle ADB = 43^\circ$ (corr. $\angle$s, $AD \parallel BC$)  

In $\triangle BCD$, by the cosine formula,  
$$CD^2 = BD^2 + BC^2 - 2 \times BD \times BC \times \cos \angle DBC$$  
$$CD = (10.689^2 + 15^2 - 2 \times 10.689 \times 15 \times \cos 43^\circ)^{\frac{1}{2}} \text{ cm}$$  
$$= 10.2 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.}$$  

24. (a) $BC = CD = 6$ cm  

In $\triangle ABC$, by the cosine formula,  
$$AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2 - 2 \times AB \times BC \times \cos \angle ABC$$  
$$AC = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2 - 2 \times 8 \times 6 \times \cos 115^\circ} \text{ cm}$$  
$$= 11.9 \text{ cm, cor. to 3 sig. fig.} \quad \boxed{11.856}$$
