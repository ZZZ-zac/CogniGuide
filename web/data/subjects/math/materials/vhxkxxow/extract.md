In each of the following, determine whether $\triangle ABC$ can be formed. If yes, find $C$. [Nos. 16–19]  

16. $A = 25^\circ$, $a = 11$ cm, $c = 27$ cm  

16. By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{27\ \text{cm}}{\sin C} = \frac{11\ \text{cm}}{\sin 25^\circ}
$$  
$$
\sin C = \frac{27 \sin 25^\circ}{11}
$$  
$$
= 1.04,\ \text{cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
> 1
$$  
$\therefore$ There is no solution for $C$.  
$\therefore$ $\triangle ABC$ cannot be formed.

17. By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{9\ \text{cm}}{\sin C} = \frac{16\ \text{cm}}{\sin 74^\circ}
$$  
$$
\sin C = \frac{9 \sin 74^\circ}{16}
$$  
$$
C = 32.7^\circ,\ \text{cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
A + C = 74^\circ + 32.732^\circ
$$  
$$
= 107^\circ,\ \text{cor. to 3 sig. fig.}
$$  
$$
< 180^\circ
$$  
$\therefore$ $\triangle ABC$ can be formed and $C = 32.7^\circ$.
