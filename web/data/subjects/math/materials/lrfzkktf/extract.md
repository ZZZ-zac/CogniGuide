25. In the figure, $ABCD$ is a quadrilateral. $AB = 5$ cm, $BC = 15$ cm,  
$CD = 12$ cm, $AC = 18$ cm and $\angle ADC = 118^\circ$.  

(a) Find $\angle DCA$. $25.9^\circ$  
(Explain)  
(b) Is the area of $ABCD$ greater than $80$ cm$^2$? Explain your answer. no  

25. (a) In $\triangle ACD$, by the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{12 \text{ cm}}{\sin \angle DAC} = \frac{18 \text{ cm}}{\sin 118^\circ}
$$  
$\angle DAC = 36.060^\circ$ or $180^\circ - 36.060^\circ$ (rejected)  
$= 36.060^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
$\angle DAC + \angle DCA + \angle ADC = 180^\circ$  
$36.060^\circ + \angle DCA + 118^\circ = 180^\circ$  
$\angle DCA = 25.9^\circ$, cor. to 3 sig. fig.  
$\boxed{25.940}$  

(b) In $\triangle ABC$,  
$s = \frac{1}{2}(5 + 15 + 18)$ cm  
$= 19$ cm  
By Heron’s formula,  
area of $\triangle ABC$  
$= \sqrt{19(19-5)(19-15)(19-18)}$ cm$^2$  
$= 32.619$ cm$^2$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
In $\triangle ACD$,  
area of $\triangle ACD$  
$= \frac{1}{2} \times AC \times CD \times \sin \angle ACD$  
$= \frac{1}{2} \times 18 \times 12 \times \sin 25.940^\circ$ cm$^2$  
$= 47.242$ cm$^2$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  

Area of $ABCD$  
$=$ area of $\triangle ABC +$ area of $\triangle ACD$  
$= (32.619 + 47.242)$ cm$^2$  
$= 79.861$ cm$^2$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
$< 80$ cm$^2$  
$\therefore$ The area of $ABCD$ is not greater than  
$80$ cm$^2$.  

Ref: DSE 2019 Paper 2 Q33  

27. In the figure, $O$ is the centre of the sector $OABC$. $D$ is a point on $OA$  
such that $OD = 2AD$. $CD$ and $OB$ intersect at $E$. $OC = 9$ cm and  
$\angle AOC = 90^\circ$. It is given that $CD$ is the angle bisector of $\angle OCB$.  

(a) Find $\angle OED$. $78.9^\circ$  
(b) Find the area of the shaded region $ABED$. $\Rightarrow$ Example 16 $20.9$ cm$^2$  

27. (a) $OA = OC = 9$ cm  
$OD + AD = OA$  
$2AD + AD = 9$ cm  
$AD = 3$ cm  
$\therefore OD = OA - AD = (9 - 3)$ cm $= 6$ cm  
In $\triangle OCD$,  
$\tan \angle DCO = \frac{OD}{OC}$  
$= \frac{6}{9}$  
$\angle DCO = 33.690^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
$\because CD$ is the angle bisector of $\angle OCB$.  
$\therefore \angle BCD = \angle DCO = 33.690^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
$\because OB = OC$  
$\therefore \angle OBC = \angle OCB$  
$= 2(33.690^\circ)$  
$= 67.380^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
In $\triangle OBC$,  
$\angle BOC + \angle OCB + \angle OBC = 180^\circ$  
$\angle BOC + 2(67.380^\circ) = 180^\circ$  
$\angle BOC = 45.240^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
In $\triangle OCE$,  
$\angle OED = \angle BOC + \angle DCO$  
$= 45.240^\circ + 33.690^\circ$  
$= \underline{78.9^\circ}$, cor. to 3 sig. fig.  
$\boxed{78.930}$  

(b) $\angle DOE = 90^\circ - \angle BOC$  
$= 90^\circ - 45.240^\circ$  
$= 44.760^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  
In $\triangle DOE$,  
$\tan \angle ODE = \frac{OC}{OD}$  
$= \frac{9}{6}$  
$\angle ODE = 56.310^\circ$, cor. to 5 sig. fig.  

By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{OE}{\sin 56.310^\circ} = \frac{6 \text{ cm}}{\sin 78.930^\circ}
$$  
$$
OE = \frac{6 \sin 56.310^\circ}{\sin 78.930^\circ} \text{ cm}
$$  
$= 5.0869$ cm  

Area of the shaded region $ABED$  
$$
= \pi \times OA^2 \times \frac{\angle AOB}{360^\circ} - \frac{1}{2} \times OD \times OE \times \sin \angle AOB
$$  
$$
= \left( \pi \times 9^2 \times \frac{44.760^\circ}{360^\circ} - \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 5.0869 \times \sin 44.760^\circ \right) \text{cm}^2
$$  
$= \underline{20.9} \text{ cm}^2$, cor. to 3 sig. fig.
