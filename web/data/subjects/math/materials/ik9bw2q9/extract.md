1. By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{x \text{ cm}}{\sin 50^\circ} = \frac{4 \text{ cm}}{\sin 30^\circ}
$$  
$$
x = \frac{4 \sin 50^\circ}{\sin 30^\circ}
$$  
$$
= \underline{6.13}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  

2. By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{x \text{ cm}}{\sin 86^\circ} = \frac{6 \text{ cm}}{\sin 60^\circ}
$$  
$$
x = \frac{6 \sin 86^\circ}{\sin 60^\circ}
$$  
$$
= \underline{6.91}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$  

3. By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{x \text{ cm}}{\sin 120^\circ} = \frac{1 \text{ cm}}{\sin 13^\circ}
$$  
$$
x = \frac{\sin 120^\circ}{\sin 13^\circ}
$$  
$$
= \underline{3.85}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
$$

$$

$$
A + 52^\circ + 63^\circ = 180^\circ

By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{x \text{ cm}}{\sin 63^\circ} = \frac{6 \text{ cm}}{\sin 65^\circ}
$$  
$$
x = \frac{6 \sin 63^\circ}{\sin 65^\circ}
$$  
$$
= \underline{5.90}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}

5.  
$$
A + B + C = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
46^\circ + B + 83^\circ = 180^\circ
$$  
$$
B = 51^\circ
$$  
By the sine formula,  
$$
\frac{x \text{ cm}}{\sin 51^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{7} \text{ cm}}{\sin 83^\circ}

$$
x = \frac{\sqrt{7} \sin 51^\circ}{\sin 83^\circ}
$$  
$$
= \underline{2.07}, \text{ cor. to } 3 \text{ sig. fig.}
