1. In $\triangle ABC$, $B = 30^\circ$, $C = 20^\circ$ and $a = 9$ cm. Solve the triangle.  
2. In $\triangle ABC$, $B = 72^\circ$, $C = 48^\circ$ and $b = 13$ cm. Solve the triangle.  
$\hookrightarrow$Ex 9A: 7–9  

$$
\begin{aligned}
& A + 30^\circ + 20^\circ = 180^\circ \quad (\text{sum of } \angle s) \\
& \therefore A = 130^\circ \\
\\
& \frac{b}{\sin 30^\circ} = \frac{9}{\sin 130^\circ} \\
& b = 5.87 \, \text{cm} \\
\\
& \frac{c}{\sin 20^\circ} = \frac{a}{\sin 130^\circ} \\
& c = 4.02 \, \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& A + 72^\circ + 48^\circ = 180^\circ \quad (\text{sum of } \angle s) \\
& \therefore A = 60^\circ \\
\\
& \frac{a}{\sin 60^\circ} = \frac{13}{\sin 72^\circ} \\
& a = 11.8 \, \text{cm} \\
\\
& \frac{c}{\sin 48^\circ} = \frac{13}{\sin 72^\circ} \\
& c = 10.2 \, \text{cm}
\end{aligned}
$$

3. In $\triangle ABC$, $A = 50^\circ$ and $B = 25^\circ$.  
Explain  
(a) Is it true that $BC = 2AC$? Explain your answer.  
(b) If $BC = 10$ cm, is it possible that $AB > 13$ cm? Explain your answer.  

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \angle C = 50^\circ + 25^\circ = 180^\circ \\
& \therefore \angle C = 105^\circ \\
\\
& \frac{BC}{\sin 50^\circ} = \frac{AC}{\sin 25^\circ} \\
& BC = 1.81 \, AC \\
& \therefore \text{False}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \frac{10}{\sin 25^\circ} = \frac{AB}{\sin 105^\circ} \\
& AB = 12.6 \, \text{cm} \\
& \therefore \text{No}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
