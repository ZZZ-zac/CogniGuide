CogniGuide

Upload your notes. Get questions written from them. Answer, get graded against your own material, and keep reviewing what you missed.

Zachary Zhang · Hugo Wong

Why CogniGuide

Re-reading notes feels productive but builds little memory; testing yourself (active recall) works far better. CogniGuide turns handwritten or digital notes into short-answer quizzes, so every session becomes self-testing on your own material.

Passive re-readingFamiliarity feels like understanding; gaps stay hidden.
Active recall with CogniGuideScores and feedback reveal what you have not mastered.

Design principles

Grounded in your notes
RAG: questions and grading are based on the original note chunks.
Level-aware
Hong Kong levels S1–S6; questions and grading adapt to the level.
Learn from mistakes
Scores below 60 are logged and reviewed until mastered.
Cloud or fully local
Runs on your own machine, no login. Use Bailian cloud models, or Ollama so notes never leave it.

How it works: from notes to mastery

01 · UPLOAD
Add notes
PDFs or photos of handwritten notes, sorted into subject folders and processed in the background.
PDF · PNG · JPG · WEBP
02 · TRANSCRIBE
Image → text
Each page is rendered to an image; a vision model transcribes text and math into Markdown + LaTeX.
qwen3-vl-plus
qwen2.5vl:3b
03 · INDEX
Chunk & embed
Text is split into ~800-character overlapping chunks, embedded and stored in LanceDB for semantic search.
text-embedding-v4
bge-m3
04 · GENERATE
Write questions
An LLM writes five short-answer questions per material, tuned to the student's level.
qwen-plus
qwen3:4b-instruct
05 · GRADE
Score & feedback
Answers are scored 0–100 against the source notes, with correct points, missed points and feedback.
qwen-plus
qwen3:4b-instruct
↺ Feedback loop: any answer scoring below 60 is saved to the Mistakes log → review rounds of five → a later score of 60 or more marks it mastered. Quick Quiz mixes questions from all subjects, optionally focused on a topic via semantic search.
